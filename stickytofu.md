# Sticky Broccoli Tofu with Noodles

Serves: 2 people

## Ingredients

### Tofu & Broccoli
- 300 g tofu
- 1 head of broccoli
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- Paprika powder
- Onion powder
- Garlic powder
- Salt
- Pepper

### Noodles
- 200 g dry noodles

### Sauce
- 1 onion
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- Vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sesame oil
- 4 tbsp vinegar
- 4 tbsp maple syrup
- 4 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Cut tofu and broccoli into bite-sized pieces. Toss with cornstarch and seasonings. Spread on a baking tray and bake at **180°C (fan oven) for ~20 minutes** until crispy.
2. Cook noodles while the tofu and broccoli are baking. Drain and rinse with cold water.
3. Mix all sauce ingredients together in a container.
4. When noodles are ready and tofu is done, heat oil in a pan. Sauté chopped onion for **1 minute**, then add garlic and sauté for another **1 minute**.
5. Add the sauce and bring to a boil. Add the baked tofu and broccoli, then let the sauce reduce slightly while keeping it saucy.
6. Add the noodles, turn off the heat, and mix for about **1 minute** using the residual heat. Serve.

## Tips
- Add **2 tbsp peanut butter** to the sauce for a richer, creamier flavor.
- Rice can be used instead of noodles. Serve the tofu, broccoli, and sauce **on top of the rice instead of mixing everything together**.
- For a poke-style bowl, add cucumber salad, avocado, and edamame beans.
- Other vegetables can be roasted together with the tofu and broccoli in the oven, such as bell peppers, carrots, or zucchini.
- For a darker and deeper-colored sauce, replace part of the regular soy sauce with **dark soy sauce**.
- **Balsamic vinegar** can be used instead of regular vinegar to make the sauce darker and add a slightly sweeter flavor.
- A little oil and soy sauce can be added to the tofu and broccoli mix before tossing with cornstarch and seasoning.
- The tofu and broccoli can be placed in a cold oven and baked for about **5 minutes longer**.
